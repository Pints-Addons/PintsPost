Pint's Postal v1.0 - a better mailbox
(WoW Classic Era / Hardcore / Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Works on its own.
  Optional:  Pint's Alts - all your characters appear in contacts right away
             (without it, each alt is added the first time you log it in)

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsPost
  Restart the game.

WHAT IT ADDS TO THE MAILBOX
  Contacts       A book button by the To: box - your alts, people you've mailed, friends and guild -
                 and names finish themselves as you type. Optionally fills in the last person you mailed.
  Open All       Opens every mail of the kinds you choose: auction cancelled / expired / outbid / sold / won,
                 and other mail with money or items. Shift-click it to open everything.
                 Mail asking for payment on delivery (COD) is never opened for you.
  Select         Tick boxes on the inbox with Open and Return buttons.
                 Shift-click a box: everything between it and the last one. Ctrl-click: every mail from that sender.
  Shortcuts      Shift-click a mail: take everything.  Ctrl-click a mail: return it.
                 Alt-click an item in your bags: attach it.
  Expiry         An icon on each mail shows whether it will come back to you or be deleted - click it to do that now.
  Copy, Forward  Copy a mail's text; forward a mail (and its items) to someone else.
  Quick attach   Buttons that attach all your cloth, leather, herbs, ore and bars, enchanting or elemental
                 materials. Right-click one to set who it goes to by default.
  Money subject  Leave the subject blank when sending gold and it fills in the amount.
  Trade block    Trades and charter requests are turned away while the mailbox is open.
  Takings        How much gold the mailbox gave you, printed when you close it.

OPTIONS
  The gear button at the top right of the mailbox, or /post. Every feature can be switched off, and the
  Open All speed and free bag slots to keep are adjustable.
  Using Postal? The options have a "Switch Postal off" button (or /post off-postal).
